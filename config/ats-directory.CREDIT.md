# Credit for `ats-directory.csv`

`ats-directory.csv` is the ATS Company Directory from LastRound AI Hiring Data, August 2026 snapshot:
9,935 companies with their public Greenhouse, Lever or Ashby job board. It is used unchanged by
`npm run explore` to look for roles beyond your watchlist.

- Source: https://datahub.io/lastroundai-hiring-data/lastroundai-hiring-data/ats-directory
- DOI: 10.6084/m9.figshare.33154145
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Some boards have closed since it was built. Explore notes those in `data/explore-dead.json` and skips them next time.
